# Dragon Quant Promo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A focused market-intelligence film for A-share short-term traders: a deep navy data-room canvas, crisp off-white typography, and one decisive signal-red accent. The motion should feel measured and analytical rather than speculative—evidence enters in layers, noisy signals resolve into a clear decision path, and the final frame leaves room for the product name and URL.

## Colors

- `#07111F` — ink / primary canvas
- `#0D1B2A` — raised surfaces and panels
- `#F4F7FA` — primary type and chart lines
- `#9AAABE` — secondary labels and inactive data
- `#E5392F` — signal red for active states and transitions

## Typography

- `Inter` for Latin labels and numbers;
- `PingFang SC`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, or the platform sans-serif fallback for Chinese copy.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not show guaranteed returns, win rates, or fabricated performance claims;
- do not use neon cyan/purple gradients or generic crypto imagery;
- do not turn every feature into an equal-size card grid;
- do not use unreadable microtext or decorative chart axes;
- do not imply that a rule signal is an automatic order.
